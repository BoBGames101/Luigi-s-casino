Luigi's Picture Poker

Luigi is back at the casino, are your coins ready?

The rules are simple: Get a better hand than luigi and win coins based on how much you bet multiplied by your hand value.

  • One Pair => x2
  • Two Pairs => x3
  • Three Of A Kind => x4
  • Full House => x6
  • Four Of A Kind => x8
  • Five Of A Kind => x16

The game is over once you can't bet anymore


Based on the picture poker minigame found in Super Mario 64 DS/New Super Mario Bros
Original rights to Nintendo
